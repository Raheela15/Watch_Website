## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an uncompromising digital salon for high-complication horology and independent master watchmaking. The visual direction balances the precision mechanics of Swiss watch engineering with avant-garde modern luxury. It rejects the dated, gilded tropes of heritage commerce in favor of an architectural, luminous obsidian environment where mechanical timepieces are displayed like rare collector artifacts suspended in deep space.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Sovereign & Rare:** The interface exudes quiet authority and discernment. Every element feels deliberate, weighted, and bespoke.
- **Micro-Mechanical Precision:** Subtle cues echo high horology—sapphire crystal anti-reflective properties, mirror-polished chamfers, dial knurling, and balance-wheel calibration.
- **Architectural Calm:** Generous visual breathing room, deliberate negative space, and deep tonal transitions evoke a hushed private showroom at midnight.

### Design Movement: Dark Glassmorphic Precision
The visual framework merges ultra-refined glassmorphism with high-contrast technical minimalism. Translucent obsidian surfaces feature dual-layer backdrop filtration, delicate hairline platinum borders, and localized incandescent highlights that simulate the refraction of a double-domed sapphire crystal.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in atmospheric nocturnal depths, accented with horological luminescent blues and brushed platinum finishes.

### Surface Hierarchy & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base (`#050814`):** The foundational obsidian-navy canvas. Absorptive, deep, and void of digital glare.
- **Elevated Glass Tier 1 (`rgba(10, 16, 34, 0.65)`):** Used for large ambient containers, sticky navigation, and background section cards.
- **Elevated Glass Tier 2 (`rgba(16, 25, 52, 0.75)`):** Component surfaces, dialogue boxes, specification trays, and product showcases.
- **Interactive Surface (`rgba(28, 42, 84, 0.45)`):** Hover, active states, and elevated nested panels.

### Accent & Metallic Tokens
- **Chrono Cobalt (`#0066FF`):** The primary kinetic accent. Inspired by heat-blued steel hands and precision laser-etched markers. Reserved for key actions, cart triggers, and verified badges.
- **Super-LumiNova Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** The luminous secondary accent. Used for chronograph readouts, active filters, status beacons, and active micro-states.
- **Platinum Silver (`#E2E8F0`):** Metallic trims, primary typography, and precision dials. Evokes rhodium-plated bridges and 904L brushed steel.
- **Subdued Ruthenium (`#64748B`):** Secondary metadata, caliber specifications, and inactive controls.

### Structural Hairlines
- **Sapphire Border Subtle:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for standard boundaries.
- **Sapphire Border Focused:** `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` for focus rings and selected complications.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances haute-horlogerie editorial tradition with surgical digital legibility. 

### Font Selection & Roles
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the classical elegance of Swiss watchmaking manufacture catalogs. Applied to marquee timepiece titles, editorial narratives, and thematic headers. Rendered predominantly in lighter weights (`400`–`500`) with subtle negative tracking.
- **Primary Body & Context (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean, contemporary legibility for case dimensions, movement descriptions, provenance notes, and narrative prose.
- **Technical & Calibration Metadata (`Inter`):** Drives all technical specifications (vibrations per hour, power reserve, jewels, reference numbers, prices, and telemetry tags). Styled with uppercase styling, medium/semibold weight, and expansive letter spacing to mimic engraved bezel indexes and movement calibers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around an expansive, fluid 12-column grid designed to spotlight high-resolution watch photography and intricate mechanical detailing.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4-column layout. Margin: `1.25rem` (`margin`). Gutter: `1.5rem`. Timepiece galleries shift to horizontal kinetic carousels; technical specs collapse into an accordion matrix.
- **Tablet (`768px – 1199px`):** 8-column layout. Margin: `2.5rem` (`margin-tablet`). Gutter: `2rem`. Two-column product grid with sticky filter ribbons.
- **Desktop (`1200px+`):** 12-column layout. Margin: `4.5rem` (`margin-desktop`). Gutter: `2.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`). Max container width capped at `1560px` to maintain viewing angles comparable to a luxury boutique vitrine.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing favors high breathing ratios (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between distinct thematic zones to prevent visual clutter and accentuate the solitary beauty of individual timepieces. Internal component clusters (e.g., dial attributes, caliber stats) adhere strictly to `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight, technical cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-optical depth inspired by anti-reflective sapphire crystals over multi-layered skeleton movements, avoiding heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers & Frosted Diffusion
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep `#050814` with a subtle, non-repeating vignette.
- **Tier 1 (Structural Trays & Navigation):** `rgba(10, 16, 34, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an interior hairline rim `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Timepiece Cards & Sheets):** `rgba(16, 25, 52, 0.6)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, anchored by an external boundary of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Flyouts, Modals, Complications):** `rgba(20, 32, 68, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(36px)` paired with a soft cobalt bloom: `box-shadow: 0 20px 50px rgba(0, 8, 30, 0.8), 0 0 30px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.12)`.

### Optical Prismatic Lighting
Cards and modular trays feature an asymmetric top-left edge glow (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 60%)`) to emulate light raking across a hand-beveled titanium watch case.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is precise, architectural, and restrained, drawing directly from stepped watch cases, cushion lugs, and sapphire bezels.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive components (buttons, input fields, tags, spec modules) leverage a strict `0.25rem` (4px) radius. This sharp, controlled edge reinforces engineering discipline and mechanical rigor.
- **Card & Showcase Shells (`rounded-lg`):** Product exhibition cards, preview drawers, and media tiles utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corner rounding.
- **System Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** Flyout drawers, concierge panels, and master modals resolve at `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Prohibited:** Completely rounded/pill contours (`rounded-full`) are strictly forbidden, except for circular dial color indicators and miniature notification jewels.

## Components

### Buttons & Commerce Triggers
- **Primary ("Acquire" / "Inquire"):** Solid `#0066FF` with text in `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`). Border: `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`. Soft inner glow with a transition to `#1A75FF` on hover accompanied by a subtle `0 0 20px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.35)` luminous halo.
- **Secondary ("Explore Caliber"):** Translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with a hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Text in `#E2E8F0`. Hover triggers a platinum sheen: background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless, zero fill. Platinum silver text with an underline indicator that transitions smoothly from `0%` to `100%` width using `#38BDF8`.

### Timepiece Display Cards
- **Structure:** Multi-layered glass surface (`Tier 2`) with an aspect ratio calibrated for vertical watch orientation. Features high-res macro photography against translucent neutral depth.
- **Bezel Badging:** Micro-pill badges in the top-right corner indicating "Unique Piece", "Certified Pre-Owned", or "Bespoke Order" using `label-sm`, tracking `0.12em`, and a subtle `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` fill with `#38BDF8` text.
- **Hover Micro-State:** Subtle image zoom (`1.03x`), delicate hairline border illumination (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`), and instant reveal of secondary technical metrics (e.g., case thickness, caliber reference).

### Chips & Filter Tokens
- **State Inactive:** Frosted glass `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#94A3B8` (`label-md`).
- **State Active:** Filled with `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)`, bordered by `#38BDF8`, text `#FFFFFF`. Features a pulsing micro-cyan diamond indicator preceding the label text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Base Input:** Background `rgba(5, 8, 20, 0.6)`. Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography `body-md` in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Active / Focus:** Border transitions to `#0066FF` with an exterior glow of `0 0 0 1px #0066FF, 0 0 16px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Placeholders:** Styled in `#64748B`, italicized or tracked out to denote subtle guidance.

### Checkboxes & Technical Selectors
- **Geometry:** 16x16px squares with `2px` corner radius. Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Checked State:** Background `#0066FF`, border-color `#38BDF8`. Centered icon is a razor-thin platinum checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Horological Spec Matrix:** A structured grid for timepiece attributes (Movement, Frequency, Jewels, Water Resistance). Uses alternating frosted banding with left-aligned `label-md` keys in `#64748B` and right-aligned `body-md` values in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Reserve Meter / Complication Dial:** A dynamic graphic component rendering remaining power reserve or limited edition count using glowing SVG arcs in `#38BDF8`.
- **Luminescence Simulator:** A floating toggle button on watch showcase pages that drops the ambient page lighting to complete obsidian (`#010206`) and illuminates the timepiece's Super-LumiNova dial markers.
